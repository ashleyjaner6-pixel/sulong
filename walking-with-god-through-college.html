    Walking With God Through College

    Ashley Janer September 20, 2026

    A Christian student's journey through pressure, purpose, and faith.

    College was something I looked forward to, but I don't think I fully understood how challenging it would be.

    As a BSIT student, there are days when school feels overwhelming. There are programming activities that don't work no matter how many times I try to fix them, deadlines that seem to come all at once, exams to prepare for, and moments when I wonder if I'm really capable of doing all of this.

    Sometimes, I get tired.

    Sometimes, I feel pressured.

    And sometimes, I question whether I'm good enough.

    But somewhere between the deadlines, sleepless nights, and stressful days, I've been learning something important:

    I don't have to go through college alone.

    When I Feel Like Giving Up

    There are moments when I just want to stop for a while.

    When a program doesn't work, when I don't understand a lesson, or when I feel like everyone else is doing better than me, it's easy to become discouraged.

    But I've slowly learned to bring those feelings to God.

    I may not always know what to do next, but I can pray.

    I may not have all the answers, but I can trust Him.

    “Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established.”
    — Proverbs 16:3

    This verse reminds me that my efforts matter, but I don't have to carry everything by myself.

    Faith and My Studies

    Being a Christian doesn't mean that college suddenly becomes easy.

    I still have to study.

    I still have to practice programming.

    I still have to submit requirements and prepare for exams.

    Faith doesn't replace hard work. Instead, it gives me a reason to keep going when the work becomes difficult.

    Before an exam, I can pray.

    Before starting an assignment, I can ask God for wisdom.

    When I make mistakes, I can remind myself that one failure doesn't define me.

    And when something finally works after hours of frustration, I can thank Him for giving me the strength to keep trying.

    Learning to Trust God's Timing

    One of the hardest things I'm learning is that my plans aren't always God's plans.

    Sometimes I want things to happen immediately.

    I want to know where my degree will take me.

    I want to know what my future career will look like.

    I want to know whether all the effort I'm putting into my studies will eventually be worth it.

    But I am learning to take things one day at a time.

    I don't need to know everything about my future to trust the One who already knows it.

    “For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD...”
    — Jeremiah 29:11

    Still Learning, Still Growing

    I'm not writing this because I have everything figured out.

    I don't.

    I'm still a student. I'm still learning programming, learning how to handle pressure, learning how to manage my time, and most importantly, learning how to grow in my relationship with God.

    There will probably be more difficult semesters.

    More difficult subjects.

    More moments of doubt.

    More nights when I feel exhausted.

    But I hope that through all of them, I remember that God is still with me.

    My degree is part of my journey, but it isn't my entire identity.

    Before I am a BSIT student, I am someone who is learning to walk with Christ.

    And whatever happens next, I want to keep moving forward with Him.

    One assignment at a time.
    One challenge at a time.
    One prayer at a time.

    And one day, I hope I can look back and say:

    “God was with me through it all.” 🤍
