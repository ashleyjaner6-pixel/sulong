Walking the Path: My Journey

Every student has a story behind their transcripts and sleepless nights. When people look at college life, they usually see the output: final grades, finished projects, graduation photos, or a completed degree. But the real story happens in the quiet, unseen spaces in between.

My journey hasn't always been a straight, perfectly planned path. There have been seasons filled with heavy academic workloads, moments of self-doubt, and times when I wondered if I was even heading in the right direction.

The Map We Didn't Draw Ourselves

Coming into Information Technology and tackling the daily rush of college life taught me one major lesson: you can plan every single detail, but things rarely go exactly according to script.

  • Writing code that breaks repeatedly until it finally clicks.

  • Managing tight deadlines for major school projects while trying to keep a balanced routine.

  • Finding out that learning isn't just about passing exams—it's about character building.

Through every hurdle, big or small, God has been the steady anchor. What felt like setbacks at the time often turned out to be redirections toward something much better.

Why I Built This Space

College Days with God started as a digital corner to document this exact season of growth. It’s a space to be honest about the messy parts of being a student while celebrating how grace shows up in everyday life.

If you are also walking through your own chaotic season of late-night deadlines, career planning, or searching for purpose, I hope this space reminds you that you aren't walking alone. We are all figuring it out, step by step, day by day.
