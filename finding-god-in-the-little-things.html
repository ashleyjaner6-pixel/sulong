Finding God in the Little Things

Ashley Janer September 20, 2026
 

Sometimes, His blessings are hidden in the ordinary.

College life can make me focus so much on what I still need to accomplish that I forget to notice what I already have.

I think about the next assignment.

The next exam.

The next deadline.

The next problem I need to solve.

Sometimes, I'm so focused on getting through the day that I forget to stop and be thankful.

But recently, I've been learning that God's goodness isn't always found in huge, life-changing moments.

Sometimes, it's found in the little things.

A Simple Day Can Still Be a Blessing

A quiet morning.

A good meal after a long day.

A friend who makes me laugh when I'm stressed.

A professor who explains something until I finally understand it.

A program that finally works after hours of debugging.

A few minutes of peace before going to sleep.

These things may seem small, but they remind me that there is still so much to be thankful for.

“This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.”
— Psalm 118:24

Every day doesn't have to be perfect for it to be a gift.

When I'm Too Focused on What's Missing

Sometimes, I compare my life to other people.

I see people who seem to have everything figured out.

Someone is already achieving something I want.

Someone seems more confident.

Someone seems further ahead.

And without realizing it, I start thinking about everything I don't have.

But comparison makes it easy to overlook God's blessings in my own life.

I have my own journey.

My own pace.

My own lessons.

And I believe God has a purpose for each season.

Thank You, Lord

So today, I want to pause and simply say:

Thank You, Lord.

Thank You for giving me another day.

Thank You for the strength to continue studying even when I'm tired.

Thank You for the people who encourage me.

Thank You for the opportunities I have.

Thank You for the lessons that challenge me.

Thank You even for the difficult moments that are teaching me to become stronger.

And thank You for staying with me through all of it.

Learning to Notice

I'm still learning how to slow down.

I'm still learning how to appreciate what I have instead of constantly worrying about what comes next.

Maybe God's blessings aren't always loud.

Maybe sometimes they're quiet.

Maybe they're hidden inside ordinary moments that I would normally overlook.

So I want to start paying more attention.

To the little joys.

The small victories.

The peaceful moments.

The answered prayers.

And even the challenges that help me grow.

Because when I look closely, I realize that God has been there all along.

In the big moments.
In the difficult moments.
And especially in the little ones. 🤍

God Is Teaching Me to Be Thankful

Lately, I've been realizing that gratitude doesn't always come naturally, especially when I'm busy with school and everything that comes with college life.

There are days when I feel tired, overwhelmed, or worried about whether I'm doing enough. But when I take a moment to look around, I realize there are already so many things God has given me that I sometimes forget to appreciate.

A simple meal.

A peaceful moment.

A friend checking up on me.

A good conversation.

Finally understanding a difficult lesson.

Even getting home safely after a long day.

These ordinary moments may not seem significant, but they are reminders that God's goodness is present even in the simplest parts of my life.

Sometimes, I think I'm waiting for a big blessing before I can truly be grateful. But maybe gratitude begins with recognizing the blessings that are already here.

“This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.”
— Psalm 118:24

College has taught me that life won't always go according to my plans. There will be stressful deadlines, difficult subjects, unanswered questions, and moments when I feel like I'm falling behind.

But I'm learning that I don't have to have everything figured out.

I can simply take one day at a time.

And in every ordinary day, I can choose to notice God's goodness.
