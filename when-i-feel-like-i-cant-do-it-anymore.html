When I Feel Like I Can't Do It Anymore

Ashley Janer September 20, 2026

Sometimes, faith means continuing even when you feel tired.

There are days when I feel like I've reached my limit.

I have schoolwork waiting for me, deadlines getting closer, and lessons that I still don't completely understand. Sometimes I sit in front of my laptop and wonder how I'm going to finish everything.

As a BSIT student, there are times when programming feels frustrating.

One error turns into another.

Something that should have taken an hour takes the whole day.

And after trying again and again, I sometimes just want to give up.

It's Okay to Be Tired

I've learned that being tired doesn't mean I'm weak.

There are days when I don't feel motivated.

There are days when I make mistakes.

There are days when I don't accomplish everything I planned.

And that's okay.

I don't have to be strong every second.

I can rest.

I can cry.

I can admit that something is difficult.

And I can bring that feeling to God.

“I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.”
— Philippians 4:13

This verse reminds me that my strength doesn't have to come entirely from myself.

When My Own Strength Isn't Enough

Sometimes I try to solve everything on my own.

I keep pushing myself because I think I should be able to handle everything.

But eventually, I realize that I need God.

I need His wisdom when I don't know what to do.

I need His peace when I'm anxious.

I need His strength when I'm exhausted.

And I need His reminder that one difficult day doesn't mean I've failed.

I Can Start Again Tomorrow

Maybe I don't have to finish everything tonight.

Maybe I just need to finish what I can.

Maybe I need to close my laptop, say a prayer, get some rest, and try again tomorrow.

Starting again isn't failure.

Sometimes, starting again is courage.

College is teaching me more than just programming and technology.

It's teaching me patience.

It's teaching me discipline.

It's teaching me how to handle failure.

And most importantly, it's teaching me how much I need God.

Keep Going

I don't know exactly how difficult the road ahead will be.

There will probably be more assignments, more exams, more difficult programs, and more moments when I question myself.

But I don't want fear to be the reason I stop.

I want to keep going.

Not because I know I can handle everything by myself, but because I know I can bring everything to God.

When I feel like I can't do it anymore, I'll remind myself:

I can rest, but I don't have to give up.

I can take one more step.

I can try one more time.

I can pray one more prayer.

And tomorrow, I can begin again.

God is still with me—even on the days when I don't feel strong. 🤍
