When College Feels Too Heavy

Ashley Janer September 20, 2026

Learning to pause, pray, and trust God in the middle of the pressure.

There are days when college feels heavier than usual.

As a BSIT student, I sometimes find myself staring at my laptop for hours, trying to understand a program that refuses to work. There are activities to finish, lessons to study, deadlines to remember, and exams waiting around the corner.

Sometimes, I start thinking:

“Kaya ko pa ba?”

I know I'm supposed to keep going, but there are moments when I feel completely exhausted.

When I Don't Know What to Do

I've learned that one of the hardest parts of being a student isn't always the workload.

Sometimes, it's the fear that comes with it.

The fear of failing.

The fear of disappointing myself.

The fear that maybe I'm not good enough.

And when those thoughts become too loud, I try to remind myself to stop for a moment.

To breathe.

To pray.

I don't always have a long prayer. Sometimes it's simply:

“Lord, please help me get through this.”

And somehow, saying those words reminds me that I don't have to carry everything alone.

God Doesn't Promise an Easy Life

Being a Christian doesn't mean that I won't struggle.

There will still be difficult subjects, stressful deadlines, mistakes, and days when I don't feel motivated.

But I believe God can meet me even in those ordinary and difficult moments.

“Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and he shall sustain thee.”
— Psalm 55:22

I love the reminder that I can bring my burdens to God.

I don't have to pretend that everything is okay when it isn't.

I can tell Him that I'm tired.

I can tell Him that I'm scared.

I can tell Him that I don't understand.

And I can trust Him with the things I cannot control.

One Step at a Time

Sometimes I think too far ahead.

I worry about graduating.

I worry about my career.

I worry about whether I'll become the person I hope to be.

But maybe I don't need to figure everything out today.

Today, I just need to do what I can.

Finish the assignment.

Study the lesson.

Fix the code.

Get some rest.

Pray.

Then do it again tomorrow.

I am slowly learning that progress doesn't always look big. Sometimes, progress is simply choosing not to give up.

If You're Struggling Too

If you're also a student who feels tired, overwhelmed, or uncertain about the future, I hope you remember this:

You don't have to have everything figured out right now.

Your current struggle is only one part of your story.

Keep doing your part, but don't forget to give your worries to God.

Study hard.

Pray harder.

Rest when you need to.

And keep going.

Because maybe the season you're struggling through today is preparing you for something you cannot see yet.

One day at a time.
One prayer at a time.
One line of code at a time.

I'm still learning.

I'm still struggling.

I'm still growing.

But I'm not walking alone. 🤍

About Me
Hi, I'm Ashley Rhiene. 🤍
I’m a BSIT student who is still learning, growing, and figuring out where God is leading me.

College has never been easy for me. As a student in Information Technology, I face programming problems, deadlines, exams, and moments when I question whether I’m really capable of doing all of this. There are days when I feel motivated, and there are also days when I feel tired and overwhelmed.

But through every season, I’ve learned that I don’t have to walk through everything alone.

My faith in Jesus has become an important part of my journey. When life feels uncertain, I find comfort in prayer and in reminding myself that God is always present, even when I cannot understand what He is doing. My relationship with Him has taught me to keep going, to be thankful for the little things, and to trust Him one step at a time.

I created this blog as a small space where I can share my experiences, reflections, struggles, lessons, and the moments where I see God working in my life.

I’m not someone who has everything figured out. I’m simply a student trying to do my best, grow in my faith, and become the person God wants me to be.

This is my journey—learning, struggling, growing, and walking with God along the way.

Still learning. Still growing. Still trusting Him. 🤍
