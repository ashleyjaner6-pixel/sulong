When God Feels Silent

Ashley Janer September 20, 2026

I learned to trust God even when I don't understand what He's doing.

There are moments in life when I pray and wait for an answer.

I ask God for guidance. I ask Him to help me with my problems, my studies, my future, and the things that worry me.

But sometimes, nothing seems to change.

The problem is still there.

The deadline is still coming.

The answer I'm waiting for hasn't arrived.

And I start wondering:

“Lord, are You listening?”

The Silence Can Be Difficult

I think one of the hardest parts of faith is waiting.

It's easy to trust God when everything is going according to plan.

It's harder when I'm praying about something and the situation doesn't seem to change.

As a student, I sometimes want immediate answers. I want to know whether I'm making the right decisions. I want to know where my degree will take me and what my future will look like.

But God doesn't always give me the answer immediately.

And I'm learning that silence doesn't necessarily mean absence.

Maybe God Is Teaching Me to Wait

Sometimes, I think God is doing something even when I can't see it yet.

Maybe the waiting is teaching me patience.

Maybe the uncertainty is teaching me trust.

Maybe the difficult season is teaching me to depend on Him instead of depending completely on myself.

“Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart.”
— Psalm 27:14

Waiting isn't easy.

But I'm learning that I don't have to understand everything to trust God.

My Future Is Still Unclear

I don't know exactly where my BSIT journey will take me.

I don't know what job I'll have after graduation.

I don't know what opportunities will come my way.

There are so many things about the future that I cannot see.

But maybe that's okay.

If I already knew everything that would happen, I wouldn't need faith.

So instead of constantly asking God to show me the entire road, I'm learning to ask Him to guide my next step.

Just the next step.

Still Here

Even on days when I feel like God is quiet, I want to remember everything He has already brought me through.

The prayers He has answered.

The doors He has opened.

The strength He gave me when I thought I couldn't continue.

The people He placed in my life.

The little blessings I sometimes forget to notice.

Maybe God isn't always silent.

Maybe sometimes I'm simply being asked to slow down enough to notice Him.

So I'll keep praying.

I'll keep studying.

I'll keep trying.

I'll keep trusting.

And even when I don't know what comes next, I'll continue walking forward with God.

I may not see the whole path, but I know who I'm walking with. 🤍
